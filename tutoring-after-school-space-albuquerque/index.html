After-school & tutoring FAQ

Can Suite B work for tutoring or an after-school program?

Yes. The open ±3,000 SF shell can be built out as classrooms, study rooms and a front desk. Program use is subject to landlord approval and any required licensing.

Is pickup easy?

Yes. There are 480 shared parking spaces, and Yale Landing is 400 feet from Kirtland AFB.

Is a tenant improvement allowance available?

Yes. TI packages are available for qualified tenants, and build-to-suit is considered. The space delivers in shell condition.
