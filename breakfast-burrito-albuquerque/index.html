Breakfast Burrito Near the Albuquerque Airport

The breakfast burrito is New Mexico's contribution to the morning, and Perico's opens at 9am on weekdays — early enough for a flight, a shift at Kirtland, or a drive up I-25.

What's in it

Egg, potato, beans and cheese, with bacon or sausage available. Order it smothered in red or green chile and eat it with a fork, which is how it's meant to be done. Chorizo with egg and cheese is the option if you want more heat.

Or go bigger

Perico's Rancheros puts two eggs, carne adovada and potatoes on a plate under red or green. Huevos rancheros comes with eggs, potatoes, beans and rice. Both come with a tortilla.

Before a morning flight

The Sunport is 1.2 miles away — about three minutes. Order ahead for pickup and eat it before you drop the rental car, which beats anything past security.

Common Questions

Good to Know

Where can I get a breakfast burrito near the Albuquerque airport?

Perico's at Yale Landing, 2500 Yale Blvd SE, opens at 9am weekdays and 10am Saturday. It's 1.2 miles from the Sunport.

How much is a breakfast burrito?

Egg, potato, beans and cheese at Perico's, with bacon or sausage available.

What time does breakfast start?

Perico's opens at 9am Monday through Friday and 10am on Saturday. Closed Sunday.
