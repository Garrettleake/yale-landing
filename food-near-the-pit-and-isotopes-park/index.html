Yale Landing/2.2 Miles from the Ballpark

Food Near The Pit & Isotopes Park

The Pit and Isotopes Park sit together about 2.2 miles north — roughly six minutes. Eat properly before the first pitch or tip-off instead of paying stadium prices, or come back afterwards for a beer without fighting the lot.

Before the game

Perico's is fast enough that you can eat and still make first pitch — burritos, tacos by the six-pack, dinner plates with rice and beans. Free parking, and you're back on Yale in minutes.

After the game

FMV Brewing Co. upstairs pours until 9pm Wednesday through Saturday and 8pm Sunday, with a patio looking toward Downtown and the Sandias. Seven New Mexico beers on tap and cocktails built on local spirits.

Bring the kids

Paletería Cuauhtémoc does fruit and cream paletas, shaved ice, milkshakes and elote until 9pm every day. It's the easiest post-game stop there is.

Good to Know

Where can I eat before an Isotopes game?

Yale Landing at 2500 Yale Blvd SE is 2.2 miles from Isotopes Park, about six minutes, with three independent places to eat and free parking.

Is there a bar near The Pit?

FMV Brewing Co. at Yale Landing is 2.2 miles away, open Wednesday through Sunday with New Mexico beer on tap.
