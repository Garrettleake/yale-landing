Yale Landing/The Yale Blvd Hotel Strip

Restaurants Near the Albuquerque Airport Hotels

There are more than 2,000 hotel rooms clustered around Yale Blvd and Gibson — Best Western, Hilton Garden Inn, Residence Inn, Sheraton, La Quinta and a dozen more. Yale Landing sits in the middle of them, which means dinner doesn't have to be the lobby bar or a chain.

Walk or drive, either works

If you're on the Yale Blvd strip you're a few minutes away by car and, from several hotels, close enough to walk. There's free parking either way, and you can eat at one place, get dessert at another and have a drink at a third without moving the car.

Good for a work trip

Perico's is fast, generous and cheap enough to expense without thinking about it — counter service. FMV Brewing upstairs is a better place to take a colleague than a hotel bar, with New Mexico beer on tap and a patio facing Downtown and the Sandias.

Feeding a group

Perico's caters — trays of enchilada casserole, posole by the gallon, tamales by the dozen. Useful if you're running a conference block or feeding a crew staying nearby.

Common Questions

Good to Know

Are there restaurants within walking distance of the Albuquerque airport hotels?

Yale Landing at 2500 Yale Blvd SE is on the hotel strip itself. From several Yale Blvd hotels it's walkable; from the rest it's a two- to five-minute drive.

Is there anywhere to get a drink near the airport hotels?

FMV Brewing Co. is upstairs at Yale Landing, pouring seven New Mexico beers plus cocktails and wine, Wednesday through Sunday.

Can I get food delivered to my hotel?

Yes — Paletería Cuauhtémoc delivers through Grubhub, and Perico's offers delivery and online ordering for pickup.
