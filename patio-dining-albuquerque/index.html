Yale Landing/A Patio at Every Storefront

Patio Dining Near the Albuquerque Airport

Albuquerque gets roughly three hundred sunny days a year, which makes an indoor table feel like a waste. Every storefront at Yale Landing has outdoor seating — pergolas, umbrellas, a fire pit, and an upstairs deck looking at the mountains.

Shade where you want it

Red steel pergolas run the length of the walkway, umbrellas cover the tables out front, and there's a covered walkway along the Alamo Avenue side. It works in July as well as October.

The upstairs deck

FMV Brewing's patio is on the second floor, facing Downtown and the Sandia Mountains — the best seat on the property at sunset, with string lights once it gets dark.

Fire pit and evening seating

There's fire pit seating with mountain views for the months when the evening turns cold, which in Albuquerque is most of them.

Common Questions

Good to Know

Where is there patio dining near the Albuquerque airport?

Yale Landing at 2500 Yale Blvd SE has outdoor seating at every storefront, including an upstairs deck at FMV Brewing with mountain views. It's 1.2 miles from the Sunport.

Is there outdoor dining with a view in Albuquerque?

FMV Brewing's upstairs patio at Yale Landing looks toward Downtown and the Sandia Mountains.

Do the restaurants have free parking?

Yes — Yale Landing has 480 shared surface spaces at no charge.
