Open latest

Paletería Cuauhtémoc runs until 9pm seven days a week — paletas, aguas frescas, milkshakes, shaved ice and snacks. It's the most reliable late option on the block.

For a drink

FMV Brewing pours until 9pm Wednesday through Saturday and 8pm on Sunday. Seven New Mexico beers, cocktails on local spirits, and a patio if the evening's warm. Closed Monday and Tuesday.

Eat before 8

Perico's closes at 8pm Monday to Friday and 7pm on Saturday, and is closed Sunday — so if you want a full New Mexican dinner, aim earlier. Order ahead for pickup if you're cutting it fine.

Common Questions

Good to Know

What food is open late near the Albuquerque airport?

Paletería Cuauhtémoc at Yale Landing is open until 9pm every day, and FMV Brewing pours until 9pm Wednesday through Saturday. Both are 1.2 miles from the Sunport.

Is anything open near the airport on Sunday?

Yes — Paletería Cuauhtémoc is open 12pm to 9pm and FMV Brewing 12pm to 8pm. Perico's is closed Sunday.

What's open right now near the Sunport?

Hours vary by business. Paletería Cuauhtémoc has the widest coverage — 11am to 9pm weekdays, 12pm to 9pm weekends.
