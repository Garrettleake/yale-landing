Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

The short version: this is a plain informational website. With one exception — the text-updates sign-up page at yalelandingabq.com/text, described in section 3 — it has no forms, no accounts, no shopping cart, no advertising and no analytics. We do not build a profile of you, and we have nothing to sell about you.

1. What we do not collect

We want to be specific, because most privacy policies are vague about this. Everything in this section applies to every page except the text-updates page at /text, which is covered in section 3. On the rest of this website there is:

  • No contact form, search box or any other input field. There is nowhere for you to type anything, so nothing is submitted to us.
  • No accounts and no logins.
  • No cookies set by this site. We do not use cookies, local storage, session storage or similar technologies for any purpose, including analytics or advertising.
  • No analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no tag manager, and no equivalent product measuring your visit.
  • No advertising or tracking pixels, and no social media tracking scripts.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information — on these pages we hold none to sell or share, and text-updates sign-ups are never sold or shared for anyone else’s marketing (section 3).

2. What is collected automatically

Web hosting

This site is served by a third-party static hosting provider. Like effectively every web server, that provider records standard technical request logs, which can include your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, and your browser’s user-agent string. These logs are generated and controlled by the hosting provider for security and operational purposes. We do not use them to identify visitors.

Fonts

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). To deliver the font files, your browser makes a request to Google, and that request necessarily discloses your IP address and user-agent to Google. Google states that Google Fonts does not set cookies and does not use these requests for advertising profiling, but the connection does happen and we would rather tell you than not. Google’s handling of that data is governed by its own privacy policy.

Apart from the sign-up widget on the text-updates page (section 3), this is the only third-party resource the site loads. Everything else — images, stylesheet, script, the leasing brochure — is served from this site itself.

3. The text-updates page (SMS sign-up)

One page, yalelandingabq.com/text, has a form. It exists so that businesses interested in leasing space at Yale Landing can ask to receive text messages about the available suites. Nothing on that page is collected unless you fill in the form and submit it.

What the form collects

Your name, your mobile phone number, an optional message, and the fact that you ticked the consent box, together with the date and time you did so. That record is kept as proof of your consent for as long as we text you and for a reasonable period afterwards.

What it is used for

To send you text messages about leasing at Yale Landing — availability, pricing, brochure links and tour scheduling — and to reply to anything you send back. The messages come from 2500 Yale, LLC, the owner of Yale Landing, and are written by its leasing broker. Frequency varies but is typically no more than four messages a month; message and data rates may apply; consent is not a condition of leasing or any purchase. Reply STOP at any time to opt out and HELP for help. Full terms are on the Text Message Terms page.

Who processes it

The form and the text-messaging service are provided by LeadConnector (HighLevel Inc.), which stores the information and delivers the messages on our behalf. It acts on our instructions and its own privacy policy governs its handling of the service. On the text-updates page only, that widget may set cookies or use local storage to make the form work. No other page does this.

What we will not do with your number

Your mobile number and your text-messaging opt-in will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Sharing with the service provider above, solely so it can deliver the messages on our behalf, is the only sharing that happens. Opt-in data and consent are never shared with anyone else.

Deleting it

Reply STOP to stop the messages. To have your name and number removed entirely, contact us at contact@yalelandingmarketing.com or the address in section 10 and we will delete the record, keeping only the minimum needed to honour your opt-out.

4. Links away from this site

Some links take you elsewhere: mapping and directions services, tenant websites, food-ordering platforms and social media. Once you follow one, you are on that company’s website, subject to their privacy policy, not this one. We have no control over and no visibility into what they collect.

5. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The only place the site collects any personal information is the text-updates sign-up (section 3), which is meant for businesses looking at commercial space; if you believe a child has used it, contact us and we will delete the record.

6. Your rights

Laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act and the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation give people rights to access, correct, delete or port their personal information, and to object to certain processing.

Apart from text-updates sign-ups (section 3), we do not collect or store personal information about visitors, so we usually hold nothing to give you, correct or delete. If you signed up for texts, or believe we hold information about you, write to us at the address below and we will respond. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy right.

7. Do Not Track

Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal. There is no agreed standard for responding to it. We do not track visitors across websites in any event, so the signal makes no difference to how this site behaves.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure. The only visitor data that exists is the text-updates sign-up list described in section 3, which is held by the service provider named there and is accessible only to the people who send the messages.

9. Changes

If this site ever adds analytics, another form, or anything else that collects information, this page will be updated before or at the same time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

10. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@yalelandingmarketing.com or to Yale Landing, 2500 Yale Blvd SE, Albuquerque, NM 87106.
