A real lunch, not a vending machine

Perico's has been family-owned in Albuquerque since 1982 and runs fast. Burritos built heavy, tacos by the six-pack, stuffed sopapillas, and chile that people drive across town for. Weekdays 9am to 8pm.

Something cold on the way back

Paletería Cuauhtémoc makes fruit and cream paletas the traditional way, plus aguas frescas, shaved ice, elote and prepared clamatos. Open until 9pm every day, which covers most shift patterns.

Team lunches and offsites

Perico's caters at scale — enchilada casserole for forty, rice and beans for seventy-five, posole by the gallon. FMV Brewing upstairs works for a team drink afterwards, Wednesday through Sunday.
