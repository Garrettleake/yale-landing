The neighbourhood

This corner of southeast Albuquerque has a large daytime population — the Sunport, Kirtland Air Force Base, Sandia National Laboratories, the VA and UNM are all within a couple of miles — and relatively few independent restaurants for the activity it supports. Yale Landing is one of the few clusters.

What's here

Perico's for Mexican and New Mexican food, family-owned since 1982. Paletería Cuauhtémoc for paletas, aguas frescas and snacks until 9pm. FMV Brewing upstairs for New Mexico beer, cocktails and house-roasted coffee.

Getting here

Yale Blvd carries about 13,970 vehicles a day and Gibson about 33,200, so it's easy to find. Free surface parking, and you can walk between all three businesses.
