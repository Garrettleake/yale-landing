A genuinely New Mexico tap list

Seven beers, every one from a New Mexico brewery: 7K IPA from Santa Fe Brewing, Elevated IPA and Azulito from La Cumbre, Double White from Marble, Dougie Style and High Plains Pilsner from Canteen. If you've flown in wanting to try New Mexican beer without driving all over the city, this is the efficient version.

Cocktails, wine and cider too

Cocktails built on New Mexico spirits — Los Poblanos gin, Red River agave, Vara rum. Wine by the glass from Mission Winery and St. Clair. Sandia cider by the can. And coffee roasted in house for whoever's driving.

Food from next door

FMV doesn't run a kitchen — they've partnered with Perico's downstairs, so you can order New Mexican food to your table while you drink.

Common Questions

Good to Know

Is there a brewery near the Albuquerque airport?

Yes — FMV Brewing Co. at Yale Landing, 2500 Yale Blvd SE Suite G, is 1.2 miles from the Sunport. Seven New Mexico beers on tap, open Wednesday through Sunday.

Where can I get craft beer near ABQ airport?

FMV Brewing pours Santa Fe Brewing, La Cumbre, Marble and Canteen — all New Mexico breweries — plus cider and wine.

Is there a bar near the Albuquerque airport hotels?

FMV Brewing is on the Yale Blvd hotel strip, upstairs with a patio, open from noon Wednesday through Sunday.

Is FMV Brewing veteran-owned?

Yes. It's a small veteran-owned Albuquerque taproom.
