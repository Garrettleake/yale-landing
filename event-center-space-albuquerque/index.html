Questions

Event centers FAQ

How big is the event space?

Suites E + F total ±12,600 SF with a 20-ft clear, column-free center span, and can be divided from ±4,000 SF. That fits a banquet floor, dance floor, games area or stage.

Is there parking for large events?

Yes. Yale Landing has 480 shared parking spaces, and about 2,000 hotel rooms are nearby for out-of-town guests. Event use is subject to landlord approval and City of Albuquerque permits; ask Ted about your plans.

Is a tenant improvement allowance available?

Yes. TI packages are available for qualified tenants, and build-to-suit is considered. The space delivers in shell condition.
