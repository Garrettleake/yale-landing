Questions

Medical & supply distribution FAQ

How close is it to the airport and I-25?

1.2 miles from the Sunport terminal and 1.9 miles from Interstate 25, with Gibson Blvd (33,200 cars a day) at the corner.

Can a warehouse and an office both fit?

Yes. The 20-ft clear center span suits pallet racking, and the front can be built out as office, showroom or will-call. A grade-level door can be added.

Is a tenant improvement allowance available?

Yes. TI packages are available for qualified tenants, and build-to-suit is considered. The space delivers in shell condition.
